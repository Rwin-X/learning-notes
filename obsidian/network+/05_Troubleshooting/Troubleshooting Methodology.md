# CompTIA Network Troubleshooting Methodology

This exact 7-step sequence is tested directly — memorize the order.

1. **Identify the problem** — gather info, question users, identify symptoms, duplicate if possible.
2. **Establish a theory of probable cause** — question the obvious, consider multiple approaches.
3. **Test the theory to determine cause** — confirm or escalate if theory isn't confirmed.
4. **Establish a plan of action** — and identify potential effects of the plan.
5. **Implement the solution** — or escalate as necessary.
6. **Verify full system functionality** — implement preventive measures if applicable.
7. **Document findings, actions, and outcomes**.

## Common Exam Traps

- "What's the FIRST step" → identify the problem, always.
- "What's the LAST step" → document findings — a huge number of test-takers skip past this and pick "verify functionality" instead.
- Documentation isn't optional or step 6 — it's the final step, after verification.
