# Roadmap

A progressive path through this vault, beginner to advanced. Each stage links onward to the next — follow it top to bottom on a first pass, then use the [[Ruby MOC]] to jump around freely afterward.

### Stage 1 — Beginner (Foundations)
Work through [[Foundations MOC]] in order, doing [[Exercises Foundations]] as you go. Finish with [[Project Number Guessing Game]] and [[Project CLI Todo App]].

### Stage 2 — Intermediate (OOP + Collections)
Move to [[OOP MOC]] and [[Collections MOC]] together — they reinforce each other. Do [[Exercises OOP]] and [[Exercises Collections]], then build [[Project Simple Bank Account System]], [[Project Library Management System (OOP)]], and [[Project Word Frequency Counter]].

### Stage 3 — Advanced (Advanced + Metaprogramming)
Study [[Advanced MOC]] and [[Metaprogramming MOC]]. Do [[Exercises Advanced]], then build the capstone: [[Project Contact Book with File Persistence]].

### Stage 4 — Ecosystem & Beyond
Round out with [[Ecosystem MOC]] — testing, tooling, gems, and Rails — to bridge from language mastery into real-world development.
