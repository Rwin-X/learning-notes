# Resource Books

## Purpose
Curated list of recommended Ruby books by learning stage.

## Explanation
A short list of well-regarded books to supplement this vault, roughly ordered by where they fit on the [[Roadmap]]: *The Well-Grounded Rubyist* (foundations→OOP), *Eloquent Ruby* (idiomatic style, pairs with [[Ruby Style Guide (Rubocop)]]), *Practical Object-Oriented Design in Ruby* (POODR, deep OOP/design), and *Metaprogramming Ruby* (pairs with [[Metaprogramming MOC]]).

## Examples
```
Beginner:      The Well-Grounded Rubyist
Intermediate:  Eloquent Ruby
OOP Design:    Practical Object-Oriented Design in Ruby (POODR)
Advanced:      Metaprogramming Ruby
```
