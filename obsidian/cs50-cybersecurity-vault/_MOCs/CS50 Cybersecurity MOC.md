# CS50's Introduction to Cybersecurity — Map of Content

Harvard course by David J. Malan. Five weeks, non-absolute framing:
security is risk vs. reward for an adversary, and cost vs. benefit for you —
always traded off against usability.

## Core thesis (repeats every week)

- [[Security as a Tradeoff]] — the idea every week returns to
- [[Threat Model]] — who is the adversary, what do they want, what can they do
- [[Defense in Depth]] — no single control is enough; layer them

## How to use this vault

Each week has a MOC note linking its atomic concept notes. Concept notes
are short, single-idea, and link sideways to related concepts in other
weeks (e.g. [[Hashing]] in Week 1 connects to [[SQL Injection]] in Week 3
via password storage). Open Obsidian's graph view filtered to this vault
to see the shape of the course — accounts and data cluster tightly around
authentication; systems and software share network/web concepts; privacy
threads through all of it.
