# Phishing

A [[Social Engineering]] attack that impersonates a trusted party (a
bank, a coworker, a real login page) via email, message, or a fake
website, to trick the target into handing over credentials or clicking a
malicious link.

Connects across weeks:
- Week 0: steals [[Usernames and Passwords]] directly, or delivers
  [[Keylogging]] malware.
- Week 3: fake login pages are a form of code/content designed to look
  legitimate — related in spirit to [[Cross-Site Scripting XSS]], where
  attacker content is disguised as trusted content.

Mitigations: [[Two-Factor Authentication 2FA]] (stolen password alone
isn't enough), [[Passkeys]] (nothing shareable to phish in the first
place — a passkey can't be typed into a fake site), user awareness.
