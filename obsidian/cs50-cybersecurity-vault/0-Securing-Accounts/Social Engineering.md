# Social Engineering

Manipulating a person — not a system — into taking an action that
compromises security: revealing a password, granting access, disabling a
control, wiring money. The umbrella category; [[Phishing]] is its most
common digital form, and [[SIM Swapping]] often relies on it too (against
carrier support staff, not the victim).

Exploits trust, urgency, authority, or fear rather than any technical
flaw. This is why [[Security as a Tradeoff]] and
[[Defense in Depth]] matter — no technical control fixes a human clicking
a malicious link, so training and layered technical backstops
(2FA, [[Server-Side Validation|validation]], monitoring) both matter.
