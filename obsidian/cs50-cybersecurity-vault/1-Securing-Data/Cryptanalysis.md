# Cryptanalysis

The study of breaking cryptographic systems — finding weaknesses in an
algorithm, implementation, or key management that let an attacker recover
plaintext, forge signatures, or otherwise defeat the system without
"fair" access to the key.

Distinct from brute-forcing a key ([[Brute-Force Attacks]]), which
doesn't require finding any flaw in the algorithm itself — cryptanalysis
specifically targets structural weaknesses. A cipher considered "broken"
usually means cryptanalysis found a shortcut meaningfully faster than
brute force.
