# FC Barcelona — 2019/20 Season

Overview note for the second brain. Links out to every player note in `Players/` and every country note in `Countries/`.

## Squad Snapshot
- **Manager (start of season):** Ernesto Valverde (sacked Jan 2020)
- **Manager (from Jan 2020):** Quique Setién
- **Captain:** [[Lionel_Messi|Leo Messi]]
- **Stadium:** Camp Nou, Barcelona

## Key Context
- Came off the 2018/19 season where they were eliminated by Liverpool in the Champions League semifinal (3-0 first leg lead collapsed 4-0 in the return leg).
- Won La Liga in 2018/19; competed for the title again in 2019/20 (lost out to Real Madrid).
- Major summer 2019 signing: [[Antoine_Griezmann]] from Atlético Madrid.
- Youth breakout: [[Ansu_Fati]] made his first-team debut aged 16.
