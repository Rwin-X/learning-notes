# Lionel Messi 🇦🇷

Captain of [[FC_Barcelona_2019|Barcelona]] in 2019/20 and the club's all-time top scorer. Won the 2019 Ballon d'Or (his 6th) on the back of another Pichichi/Golden Shoe-winning La Liga campaign.

## Notes
- Product of La Masia, Barça's youth academy, since age 13.
- Played primarily as a false 9 / right-sided forward this season.
- Linked heavily with [[Luis_Suarez]] and [[Antoine_Griezmann]] in attack — the "MSG" front line.
