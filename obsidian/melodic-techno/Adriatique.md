# Adriatique

## Overview
<cite index="3-1">The Swiss duo bring a sophisticated, cinematic quality to melodic techno. Their album "Nude on Siamese" showed the genre's album-length potential, and their DJ sets are considered masterclasses in long-form storytelling — representing the introspective side of the genre, the 3 AM headphone listen rather than just the festival peak.</cite>
