# Rough Timeline

Approximate, not authoritative — genres bleed into each other continuously. Useful as a secondary navigation path through the vault.

- **Late 1980s** — [[Detroit Techno]] emerges (Derrick May, Juan Atkins, Kevin Saunderson)
- **Early 1990s** — Techno spreads to Europe; [[Berlin Techno]] scene forms around Hard Wax
- **1993** — [[Basic Channel]] founded in Berlin; [[Dub Techno]] coalesces
- **1995** — [[Chain Reaction]] label launches, extends dub techno's reach
- **1990s** — [[Minimal Techno]] and [[Progressive House]] develop in parallel
- **Mid-1980s onward** — [[Deep House]] develops as its own foundational strand
- **2000s** — [[Afro House]] scene grows; dub techno globalizes via Deepchord/Echospace
- **2006** — [[Diynamic]] founded by Solomun
- **2016** — [[Afterlife]] founded by [[Tale Of Us]]
- **Late 2010s** — [[Melodic Techno]] splits off from harder techno, led by [[Tale Of Us]] and [[Stephan Bodzin]]; [[Innervisions]] and Afterlife become the genre's anchor labels
- **2018+** — [[Organic House]] emerges as a downtempo offshoot; global surge in [[Afro House]] crossover
- **2020s** — [[Anyma]], [[Colyn]], [[Innellea]] establish themselves within melodic techno's second wave
- **2025–2026** — Melodic House & Techno becomes one of Beatport's biggest combined-genre years, led by [[Anyma]], [[RÜFÜS DU SOL]], and [[Afterlife]]/[[Anjunadeep]]
