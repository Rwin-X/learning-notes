# Afterlife

## Overview
<cite index="9-1">Founded by Tale Of Us (Anyma & MRAK), Afterlife is known for genre-defining sound design, cinematic builds, deep emotion, and massive drops.</cite> <cite index="3-1">The label didn't just popularize melodic techno — it defined its visual and sonic identity, extending into large-scale audiovisual production design at its events.</cite> <cite index="2-1">Afterlife parties at Berghain in Berlin are considered a signature fixture of the genre's live scene.</cite>
