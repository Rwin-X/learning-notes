# Chain Reaction

## Overview
<cite index="15-1">Launched in 1995 by Basic Channel's Moritz von Oswald and Mark Ernestus, Chain Reaction provided a platform for artists to explore dub techno's boundaries.</cite> <cite index="2-1">The label released records by Monolake, Porter Ricks, Vainqueur, Scion, and Substance</cite>, making it as foundational to dub techno as Basic Channel itself.
