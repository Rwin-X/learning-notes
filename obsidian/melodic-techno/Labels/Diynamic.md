# Diynamic

## Overview
<cite index="7-1">Founded in 2006 by German DJ/producer Solomun alongside partner Adriano Trolio, Diynamic is based in Hamburg and known for deep, emotive house and techno sounds. The label has built a reputation for high-quality releases and a distinctive blend of melodic and rhythmic elements, becoming a prominent name in the electronic scene.</cite>
