# Innervisions

## Overview
<cite index="8-1">One of the labels, alongside Afterlife, that became known for championing melodic techno's more emotionally deep, harmonically-focused sound as the genre split off from harder techno in the late 2010s.</cite> Roots trace back further into [[Deep House]] before the label's sound drifted toward melodic techno.
