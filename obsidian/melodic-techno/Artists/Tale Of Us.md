# Tale Of Us

## Overview
<cite index="3-1">Tale of Us — the Italian duo of Carmine Conte and Matteo Milleri — are the architects of modern melodic techno. Their label Afterlife didn't just popularize the genre, it defined its visual and sonic identity.</cite>

Widely regarded as the genre's founding act, alongside [[Stephan Bodzin]].
