# Basic Channel

## Overview
<cite index="16-1">Founded in 1993 in Berlin by Moritz von Oswald and Mark Ernestus, Basic Channel emerged as the cornerstone of dub techno through self-distributed vinyl releases such as the inaugural BC 01 pressing, emphasizing limited editions and a raw, immersive sound palette. The project prioritized analog warmth and dub-inspired echo effects, distributing exclusively through their Hard Wax store to foster an underground community.</cite> <cite index="15-1">Their tracks "Phylyps Trak" and "Quadrant Dub II" became emblematic of the dub techno sound, featuring heavy basslines, expansive echo effects, and a minimalist structure.</cite>
