# Stephan Bodzin

## Overview
<cite index="3-1">Bodzin is the veteran's veteran of melodic techno — a live performer who builds entire sets from hardware, creating melodic techno with a rawness and authenticity that software-only producers can't match. His sets at Time Warp, Sonar, and Awakenings are considered legendary reference points for the genre's emotional depth.</cite>

## Genre
- [[Melodic Techno]] — credited (with [[Tale Of Us]]) as one of the artists who pushed techno toward melodic content in the late 2010s
