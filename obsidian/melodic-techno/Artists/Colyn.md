# Colyn

## Overview
<cite index="3-1">One of the newer names in melodic techno, Colyn quickly became an Afterlife staple with tracks that feel both massive and intimate. His signature synth work — warm, wide, and beautifully layered — gives his productions an instantly recognizable quality, and in 2025-2026 he has been one of the most-booked artists in the scene.</cite>
