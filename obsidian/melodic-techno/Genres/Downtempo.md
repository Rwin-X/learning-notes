# Downtempo

## Definition
A broad umbrella for slower, non-club-tempo electronic music built for listening rather than dancing — the connective tissue between [[Dub Techno]], [[Ambient Techno]], and [[Organic House]] on the chill/headphone end of this whole cluster.

## Defining Characteristics
- Tempo: roughly 70–110 BPM, often with no strict four-on-the-floor pulse
- Emphasis on texture, atmosphere, and sample/field-recording work over rhythm-driven groove
- Frequently the "closing set" or "sunrise set" register at festivals adjacent to the melodic techno scene
