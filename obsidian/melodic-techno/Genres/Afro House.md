# Afro House

## Definition
House music built around African percussion traditions and rhythmic patterns, blended with deep/tribal house production. Distinct scene from melodic techno historically, but the two have merged heavily on festival lineups and in production style since the late 2010s.

## Defining Characteristics
- Tempo: roughly 115–124 BPM
- Polyrhythmic percussion, congas, shakers layered over a four-on-the-floor base
- Chant/vocal elements common
- Frequently blended live with [[Organic House]] sets
