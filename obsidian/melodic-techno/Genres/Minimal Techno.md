# Minimal Techno

## Definition
A stripped-down, repetition-focused techno style that favors subtle variation over melodic development — sonically almost the opposite pole from [[Melodic Techno]], though both descend from the same Detroit/Berlin techno root.

## Defining Characteristics
- Tempo: roughly 125–135 BPM
- Sparse arrangements, minimal harmonic content, emphasis on groove/texture over melody
- <cite index="10-1">Key figures include Ricardo Villalobos and Matthew Herbert, especially in the microhouse offshoot</cite>
- Berlin's Hard Wax record shop was a key hub for the style's early development

## Subgenres / Offshoots
- **Microhouse** — fusion of minimal techno and deep house; <cite index="10-1">known for intricate rhythms, subtle melodies, and a warm, organic sound</cite>
- [[Dub Techno]] — <cite index="10-1">a blend of minimal techno and dub reggae, characterized by deep basslines, spacious soundscapes, and reverberant textures</cite>

## Key Labels
- **BPitch Control** — Ellen Allien's label, a driving force in the genre for over two decades (per genre reference sources)
