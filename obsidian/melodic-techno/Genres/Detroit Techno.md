# Detroit Techno

## Definition
The genre's founding movement — the historical root that every genre in this vault ultimately traces back to.

## History
<cite index="15-1">Techno music emerged in the late 1980s in Detroit, reflecting the city's industrial landscape and economic challenges. Pioneers including Derrick May, Juan Atkins, and Kevin Saunderson shaped the genre, infusing elements of house music with faster, darker beats. By the early '90s, techno had spread to Europe, with Germany becoming a central hub.</cite> <cite index="15-1">The 1990s saw a second wave of Detroit producers, including Carl Craig, Stacey Pullen, and Kenny Larkin, extend the sound further.</cite>
