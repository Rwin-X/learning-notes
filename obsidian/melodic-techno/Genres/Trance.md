# Trance

## Definition
A faster, more euphoric cousin of [[Progressive House]] — included here mainly as a genealogical reference point since progressive house (and by extension [[Melodic Techno]]) shares some of its build-and-release arrangement DNA with trance.

## Related Genres
- [[Progressive House]] — closest relative
- [[Melodic Techno]] — distant, via shared arrangement philosophy
