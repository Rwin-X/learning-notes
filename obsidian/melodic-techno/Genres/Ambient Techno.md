# Ambient Techno

## Definition
Techno's structure and repetition combined with ambient music's textural, non-narrative approach to sound — beat-driven but designed for immersive listening as much as movement. Sits at the intersection of [[Dub Techno]] and pure ambient, and overlaps with the darker/introspective side of [[Melodic Techno]].

## Defining Characteristics
- Tempo: roughly 100–125 BPM, often with a soft or implied kick rather than a hard-hitting one
- Long evolving pad textures, minimal percussion variation
- Strong lineage crossover with [[Dub Techno]] artists (Deepchord, Echospace, Monolake)
