# Deep House

## Definition
One of the foundational house subgenres — built on jazzy chords, warm basslines, and a laid-back groove. Deep house is an ancestor to much of the melodic/organic house continuum; several melodic-techno-adjacent labels (Innervisions among them) came up through deep house roots before drifting toward the melodic-techno sound.

## Defining Characteristics
- Tempo: roughly 118–125 BPM
- Warm, jazz- and soul-influenced chords
- Understated, groove-first arrangements (less dramatic than melodic techno's cinematic builds)
