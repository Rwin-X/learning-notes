# Berlin Techno

## Definition
The scene, not strictly a sonic subgenre — Berlin became techno's second capital after Detroit and incubated several of the sonic branches in this vault, from stripped-down minimal techno to the deep, echo-laden dub techno sound.

## History
<cite index="15-1">Berlin's Hard Wax record shop, founded by Mark Ernestus, played a crucial role in the global scene. Ernestus and Moritz von Oswald, working as Basic Channel, developed the dub techno sound out of this Berlin context.</cite> The city's club ecosystem (Berghain among the most iconic) later became a key venue for the melodic techno wave as well — Afterlife has staged parties there.
