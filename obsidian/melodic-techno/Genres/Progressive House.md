# Progressive House

## Definition
An older lineage than melodic techno — house music built around gradual, evolving arrangements rather than obvious drops, prioritizing a slow-building "journey" over immediate dancefloor peaks. Melodic techno inherited much of its long-build, emotionally-arced structure directly from this genre.

## Defining Characteristics
- Tempo: roughly 122–128 BPM
- Long intros/outros built for DJ mixing
- Gradual filter sweeps and layering instead of hard drops
- Historically rooted in UK/early-90s house evolution, later revived by the 2010s melodic wave
