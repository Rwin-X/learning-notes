# Organic House

## Definition
A downtempo, earthy offshoot that strips away four-on-the-floor club energy in favor of live instrumentation, hand percussion, and acoustic textures — the "sunset stage" cousin of [[Melodic Techno]]. Shares the genre's love of organic sound design but trades club intensity for warmth and space.

## Defining Characteristics
- Tempo: roughly 90–118 BPM, often without a strict techno kick pattern
- Live instrumentation: guitar, hand drums, flutes, vocal chants
- Built for daytime/sunset sets rather than peak-time club floors
- Strong overlap in artist rosters with [[Afro House]]

## Related Genres
- [[Melodic Techno]] — shares organic textural language
- [[Afro House]] — closely related, often co-programmed on the same lineups
- [[Deep House]]
- [[Downtempo]]
