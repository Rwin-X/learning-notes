# Melodic House

## Definition
The house-tempo sibling of [[Melodic Techno]] — same emphasis on emotional melody, atmosphere, and long-form arrangement, but sitting at a slightly lower, groove-driven tempo and typically built on a house rhythm foundation rather than a techno one.

## Defining Characteristics
- Tempo: roughly 118–124 BPM
- Warmer low end, more swing in the groove than melodic techno
- Frequently blurs into [[Organic House]] and [[Deep House]]
- Beatport charts it jointly with [[Melodic Techno]] under "Melodic House & Techno"
