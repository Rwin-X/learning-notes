## How to Use This Vault
1. Start at a genre note — each links to its defining artists, labels, and 2-3 key tracks.
2. Artist notes link back to genre(s), label(s), and notable tracks.
3. Label notes list roster artists and genre focus.
4. [[Production Techniques MOC]] collects the sound-design and arrangement concepts referenced across genre notes.
5. Let Graph View do the rest — genres cluster naturally once enough notes exist.
