# Cinematic Builds & Breakdowns

## Definition
Long-form arrangement technique: extended breakdowns and gradual, dramatic builds (often 32+ bars) designed to create an emotional arc rather than a quick drop — the structural backbone of the melodic techno "journey" sound.

## Used In
- [[Melodic Techno]]
- [[Melodic House]]
- [[Progressive House]] — historical origin of the technique in this cluster
