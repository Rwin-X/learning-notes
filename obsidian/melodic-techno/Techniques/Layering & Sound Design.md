# Layering & Sound Design

## Definition
The practice of stacking multiple textural layers — organic (field recordings, live percussion, acoustic instruments) and synthetic (pads, arps, textures) — to build the immersive, "journey" quality central to [[Melodic Techno]] and its neighbors.

## Used In
- [[Melodic Techno]] — signature technique; wind chimes, percussion, and field recordings layered under synths
- [[Organic House]]
- [[Afro House]]
- [[Ambient Techno]]

## Notable Practitioners
- [[Colyn]] — noted for warm, wide, layered synth work as a genre signature
