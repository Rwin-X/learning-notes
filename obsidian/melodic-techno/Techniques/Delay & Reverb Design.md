# Delay & Reverb Design

## Definition
<cite index="13-1">Using extensive, often heavily processed (filtered, modulated, distorted rather than clean) reverb and delay to build deep, spacious, immersive sonic environments — the defining production signature of dub techno.</cite>

## Used In
- [[Dub Techno]] — foundational, genre-defining technique
- [[Ambient Techno]]
- [[Downtempo]]

## Notable Practitioners
- [[Basic Channel]] — originators of the technique's use in techno
