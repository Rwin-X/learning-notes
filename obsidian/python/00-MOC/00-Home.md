## How this vault works
- Notes live in folders `01` through `06`, ordered **easy → hard**.
- Every note is tagged by difficulty: `#easy` `#medium` `#hard` `#expert`, plus topic tags like `#syntax`, `#oop`, `#networking`, `#security`.
- Open **Graph View** (Ctrl/Cmd+G) and use the color groups below to see difficulty clusters visually.
- Follow the [[00-Learning-Path-MOC]] for the recommended order.
