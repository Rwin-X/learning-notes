# Web Scraping (BeautifulSoup)

`🟠 HARD` #hard

## What it covers
Parsing HTML with `BeautifulSoup` and `lxml`.

## Key points
- `soup.find_all("a", href=True)` to pull all links
- Respect `robots.txt` and rate-limit your requests — scraping ethics matter
- Pairs naturally with your OSINT-style projects (like `inOs`)

## Practice
Scrape a page's `<title>` and all outbound links, dedupe with a `set`.
