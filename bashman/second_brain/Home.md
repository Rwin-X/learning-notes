# 🐚 Bash — Zero to Hero

Second brain for the *bash.sh* course. Five levels, 80 concepts, 15 projects, one cheat sheet.

## Start here
1. Work through [[01 Levels/01 Foundation|01 Foundation]] → [[01 Levels/05 Real World|05 Real World]] in order.
2. Open each concept note, read it, then **build something** with it before moving on.
3. Use [[02 Projects]] to apply what you learned — matched by level in the roadmap below.
4. Keep [[Bash Cheat Sheet]] pinned for quick lookups.
