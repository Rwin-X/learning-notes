Each cell holds a value from 0 to 1. A ring-shaped neighborhood decides whether it grows or shrinks. Click to place a glider, or switch the pointer to paint or erase. Try nudging mu and sigma: a narrow band between an empty world and a flooded one is where creatures live. Keys: space pauses, 1 to 3 pick a pointer tool, C colony, R soup, S saves a frame, H hides the interface.
